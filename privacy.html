Privacy policy

Last updated 14 September 2026 · Version 1.0

Valheim Food Planner is a free fan tool for picking food in the game Valheim. It has no accounts, no forms, no advertising and no tracking profiles. This page covers buildapp.se/valheim only. The buildapp.se privacy policy covers the directory page.

Who is responsible?

The data controller is Patrik Löfgren, Umeå, Sweden. Contact kontakt@orgutveckling.se with questions or to exercise your rights.

What data is processed?

The planner is a static page. Everything it calculates happens in your browser. Data still arises when the page is delivered:

  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the site. In that step, IP address, time, requested address and browser information are processed.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics provides aggregated visit statistics: page viewed, country, browser, referring page and load time. Cloudflare injects a small script (beacon.min.js from static.cloudflareinsights.com). It sets no cookies, builds no device profile and does not store your IP address.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the files and keeps standard technical logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the title typeface. Your IP address goes to Google when your browser fetches it.

No profiling or automated decision-making with legal or similarly significant effects takes place.

Cookies and storage on your device

The planner sets no cookies, so there is no cookie banner.

Your settings are kept in your browser's localStorage under one key, valheim-food-planner:v1: the biome you picked, resources you unticked, your gather list and your view options (sorting, combos, feasts, per biome). This data never leaves your device and no server receives it. The storage is necessary for features you use yourself, so it needs no consent. Remove it by clearing site data for buildapp.se in your browser.

Purposes and legal bases

  • Delivery, security and operation: legitimate interest in the page working, being reachable and being protected against abuse.
  • Visit statistics: legitimate interest in knowing whether the tool is used and how fast it loads. The statistics are aggregated and cannot be linked to you.
  • Your saved settings: providing the features you use. The data stays on your device.
  • Support: answering your request if you write to the contact address.

Retention

  • Saved settings stay in your browser until you clear site data.
  • Technical logs at Cloudflare and GitHub follow their own routines, normally days to weeks.
  • Support email is normally deleted no later than 12 months after the matter is closed.

Providers and transfers outside the EU/EEA

Cloudflare, GitHub and Google are US companies and may process data outside the EU/EEA. Their data protection terms and approved transfer mechanisms, such as the Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses where applicable, apply to these transfers.

Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction or data portability, and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Requests are handled manually through the contact address above. You may also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).

Changes

Material changes are published here with a new date. If a new purpose requires consent, it is requested before processing begins.
