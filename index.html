Cookies

This site sets no cookies. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

Stored in your browser

Your settings live in your browser's local storage under one key, valheim-food-planner:v1: chosen biome, unticked resources, your gather list and view options. It never leaves your device and no server sees it. Clear it by deleting site data for buildapp.se in your browser.

Accounts and forms

None. The site never asks who you are.

Third parties your browser talks to

  • Cloudflare delivers and protects the site, and runs Cloudflare Web Analytics. Its small script counts page views, country, browser and load time in aggregate. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device and keeps no profile of you. Your IP address passes through Cloudflare to deliver the page.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the files and keeps standard technical logs.
  • Google Fonts serves the title font. Fetching it sends your IP address to Google.

No advertising, no selling of data, no profiling.
